# OPRC Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Pledge
In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming research environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the **Open Physics Research Collective (OPRC)** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 2. Standards of Scientific Integrity
Per Section 5.2 of the OPRC Charter, all members must adhere to the highest standards of scientific discourse:
- **Radical Transparency:** Disclose all data, code, and potential conflicts of interest.
- **Constructive Critique:** Peer reviews must be signed, evidence-based, and focused on technical merit.
- **Intellectual Honesty:** Acknowledge theoretical errors promptly and cite all influences via the OPRC Attribution Protocols.
- **Anti-Obstructionism:** Do not use the RFC process to block research for non-technical or personal reasons[cite: 2].

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior
The following behaviors are considered in violation of our collective standards:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks[cite: 5].
- Public or private harassment[cite: 5].
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission (doxing)[cite: 5].
- Scientific misconduct, including data fabrication, plagiarism, or patenting fundamental principles discovered within the collective[cite: 1, 4].

## 4. Enforcement and Mediation
Per Section 2.3 of the Charter, disputes regarding conduct shall be handled by the **Technical Arbitration Council**.
- **Reporting:** Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a confidential issue or contacting a Cluster Lead[cite: 5].
- **Mediation:** The Council will review the case based on blind evidence where possible[cite: 1].
- **Resolution:** Consequences for violating these standards may include temporary suspension or permanent expulsion from the OPRC repositories[cite: 5].
