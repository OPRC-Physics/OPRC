# Open Physics Research Collective (OPRC)
> "E pur si muove."

The **Open Physics Research Collective (OPRC)** is a decentralized, unincorporated association of researchers dedicated to radical transparency and open-source methodology across all domains of physics.

[📄 View Founding Charter](./Founding_Charter.pdf)

---

## 🌌 Our Mission
We aim to accelerate physical discovery by removing institutional barriers to information. Every derivation, dataset, and simulation produced by the OPRC is released under open licenses, ensuring the common heritage of physical laws remains available to all[cite: 1, 4].

## 🏗 Research Clusters
The collective is organized into distributed clusters to facilitate deep-dive inquiry:
- **High-Energy & Particle (HEPP):** Collider phenomenology and QFT.
- **Quantum Systems:** Quantum information, computing, and many-body physics.
- **Cosmos & Gravity:** Relativity, astrophysics, and dark matter.
- **Matter & Materials:** Condensed matter and thermodynamics.
- **Complexity:** Chaos theory, biophysics, and fluid dynamics.

## 🤝 How to Contribute
We follow a **"Rough Consensus and Working Code"** governance model.

1. **Observer:** Join the discussions in our Issues and Discussions tabs.
2. **Contributor:** Submit Pull Requests for code, proofs, or literature reviews.
3. **RFC Process:** Major initiatives require a *Request for Comments* prior to merging.

Please read our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.
